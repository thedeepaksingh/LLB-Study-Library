## Writing template
1. State the constitutional issue precisely.
2. Quote or accurately summarise the relevant Article.
3. Give the governing constitutional test and one leading case.
4. Apply the test to each material fact.
5. State the result and any limitation/qualification.

Do not merely provide a list of cases. Explain the rule each case contributes.
