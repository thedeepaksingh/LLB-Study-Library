# K-1002 — Official Syllabus and Sources

## Identity
- Paper: **Constitutional Law of India–I (Nature of the Constitution and Fundamental Rights)**
- Code: **K-1002**
- University/programme: CCSU, three-year LL.B.
- Semester: 1

## CCSU syllabus topics
1. Nature of the Indian Constitution, with special emphasis on **federal structure** and **form of government**.
2. Preamble of the Constitution.
3. Fundamental Rights:
   - General scheme: who can claim them, against whom they are available, and suspension/enforcement during emergencies.
   - Right to Equality: Articles 14–18.
   - Freedom of speech and expression: Article 19(1)(a).
   - Protection in respect of conviction for offences: Article 20.
   - Life and personal liberty: Article 21.
   - Right to education: Article 21A.
   - Rights against exploitation: Articles 23–24.
   - Freedom of religion: Articles 25–28.
   - Cultural and educational rights of minorities: Articles 29–30.
   - Constitutional remedies: Article 32.
4. Directive Principles of State Policy: importance and relationship with Fundamental Rights.
5. Fundamental Duties.

The numbering above is reorganised for easy navigation; the subject matter follows the university syllabus. Check the notified version applicable to your batch.
