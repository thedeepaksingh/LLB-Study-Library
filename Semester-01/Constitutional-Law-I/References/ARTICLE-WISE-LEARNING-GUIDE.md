# K-1002 — Article-Wise Learning Guide

**This is a plain-English map, not verbatim constitutional text.** Open the current Constitution of India before quoting an Article in an answer.

| Provision | Subject | What to remember |
|---|---|---|
| Preamble | Objectives and source | Sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic republic; justice, liberty, equality, fraternity |
| Arts. 12–13 | State; laws inconsistent with FR | Identify the State and the constitutional effect on inconsistent laws |
| Arts. 14–18 | Equality | Equality/equal protection, specified anti-discrimination guarantees, public employment, abolition of untouchability, titles |
| Art. 19(1)(a), 19(2) | Speech/expression | Citizens' right plus only the constitutionally listed reasonable restrictions |
| Art. 20(1) | Ex post facto | No retrospective criminalisation or greater retrospective penalty in the constitutionally prohibited sense |
| Art. 20(2) | Double jeopardy | Prosecution and punishment for the same offence, as constitutionally interpreted |
| Art. 20(3) | Self-incrimination | Accused cannot be compelled to be a witness against themselves |
| Art. 21 | Life and personal liberty | Procedure must meet constitutional standards including fairness under *Maneka Gandhi* |
| Art. 21A | Education | Free and compulsory education for children aged 6–14, implemented by law |
| Art. 23 | Trafficking/forced labour | Prohibits specified exploitation; may reach private actors |
| Art. 24 | Child employment | Under-14 children not to be employed in factories, mines or hazardous employment specified by Article |
| Arts. 25–28 | Religion | Individual conscience, denominational rights, taxes, and institutional religious instruction |
| Arts. 29–30 | Culture/minority education | Conservation of culture and rights of religious/linguistic minorities to educational institutions |
| Art. 32 | Supreme Court remedy | Move the Supreme Court to enforce Fundamental Rights; writs/orders/directions |
| Art. 226 | High Court writ jurisdiction | FR enforcement and other purposes within constitutional jurisdiction |
| Arts. 36–51 | DPSPs | Non-justiciable but fundamental in governance under Art. 37 |
| Art. 51A | Fundamental Duties | Duties of citizens; interpretation/context, not usually standalone private claims |
| Art. 358 | Emergency and Art. 19 | Specific effect during emergency due to war/external aggression; read post-44th Amendment text |
| Art. 359 | Suspension of enforcement | Presidential order affecting court enforcement of specified rights; Arts. 20 and 21 are excluded |

## Writ quick guide
- Habeas corpus: unlawful detention.
- Mandamus: public/legal duty.
- Prohibition: restrain proceeding beyond jurisdiction.
- Certiorari: quash for recognised legal/jurisdictional error.
- Quo warranto: authority to hold public office.

Always test whether jurisdiction, standing, the right's applicability, and any express constitutional limitation have been satisfied.
