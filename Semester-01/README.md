## How the materials are organised

Each subject has a syllabus/source register, revision plan, complete teaching notes, relevant legal/doctrine guide, case-law illustrations, original practice questions with worked answers, completeness matrix and changelog. The content is written for beginners in simple English and is intended to be read directly, not merely used as a list of external links.

## Evidence and accuracy safeguards

- Course codes/titles are mapped to the CCSU three-year LL.B. Semester 1 scheme found in the university's January 2026 exam schedule and CCSU-hosted syllabus PDFs.
- The specific applicability of that scheme to your admission batch still needs confirmation against your own notified syllabus.
- “Worked practice” means original study hypotheticals, not verified past-year questions (PYQs), unless an entry explicitly shows it was checked against the original question paper.
- “Expanded draft” does not mean independently verified or legally perfect. Statutes, amendments, case holdings and precise citations must be checked before formal use.
- The BNS source-preservation archive is a separate feature-branch proposal; it is not represented as merged to `main`.
