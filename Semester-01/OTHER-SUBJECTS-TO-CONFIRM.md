# Other Semester 1 Subjects — To Confirm

The library is intended to cover every subject in the applicable LL.B. course. This register prevents us from guessing subject names or paper codes.

Before creating a subject folder, confirm:
- programme type (three-year LL.B. or integrated law degree);
- admission batch and academic session;
- exact paper title and code in the applicable CCSU syllabus;
- official syllabus URL and date checked;
- whether CCSU has issued any amendments.

Once verified, create a separate folder for the subject with a dashboard, syllabus, topic notes, question bank, model answers, past-paper verification register, case-law or reference index as applicable, and a changelog. Then link it from Semester-01/README.md and MASTER-INDEX.md.

Do not merge different subjects into BNS-1 merely because they are in the same semester.
