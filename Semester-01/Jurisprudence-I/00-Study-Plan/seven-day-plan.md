# K-1001 — Seven-Day Study Plan

| Day | Topic | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Definition, nature, scope and importance of jurisprudence | 1 definition answer; list of jurisprudence's functions |
| 2 | Classical Natural Law; Stammler and Kohler | Compare classical natural law with the revival theories |
| 3 | Analytical School: Austin, Kelsen and Hart | Comparison table covering command, Grundnorm and primary/secondary rules |
| 4 | Historical School: Savigny and Maine | Explain Volksgeist and status-to-contract, with limits |
| 5 | Sociological School: Pound and Duguit | Social engineering, interests and social solidarity |
| 6 | American Modern Realism; Marxist/economic approach | Compare realism and Marxist analysis; prepare criticism points |
| 7 | Whole-paper revision and mock answers | One timed long answer, two medium answers, five short notes |

## Daily routine (about 3–4 focused hours)
1. Read the day's section in the complete notes.
2. Close the notes and explain the theory aloud in your own words.
3. Make a one-page chart: thinker, proposition, example, criticism.
4. Write an answer without copying the notes.
5. Check the doctrine guide and add missing or confused concepts to an error log.

## Essential comparison pairs
- Natural law v analytical positivism.
- Austin v Kelsen v Hart.
- Savigny v Maine.
- Pound v Duguit.
- Legal realism v formalism.
- Marxist approach v liberal/positivist accounts of law.
