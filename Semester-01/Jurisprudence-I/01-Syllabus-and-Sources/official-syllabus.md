# K-1001 — Syllabus and Source Register

## Course identity
- **University:** Chaudhary Charan Singh University (CCSU), Meerut
- **Programme assumed:** Three-year LL.B., Semester 1
- **Paper:** Jurisprudence–I (Legal Theory)
- **Paper code:** K-1001
- **Status:** Working syllabus basis verified against a CCSU-hosted syllabus and the university's January 2026 first-semester examination schedule. Always follow the syllabus notified for your admission batch.

## Topics in the CCSU syllabus
1. **Introduction:** definition, nature and scope of jurisprudence; importance of studying jurisprudence.
2. **Natural Law School:** classical natural law; revival of natural law, including Rudolf Stammler and Kohler.
3. **Analytical School:** John Austin, Hans Kelsen and H.L.A. Hart.
4. **Historical School:** Friedrich Karl von Savigny and Sir Henry Sumner Maine.
5. **Sociological School:** background and characteristics; Roscoe Pound and Léon Duguit.
6. **American Modern Realism.**
7. **Economic Approach of Law:** Marxist economic approach to law.

The official syllabus document numbers some schools inconsistently. This learning guide groups each topic under clear headings without changing the prescribed subject matter.

## Boundaries
This paper is **Jurisprudence–I (Legal Theory)**. Do not automatically add the separate Semester 2 paper Jurisprudence–II (Legal Concepts) as if it were part of K-1001. Topics such as rights, possession, ownership and liability may be used for brief illustrations, but the core coverage here follows the seven syllabus headings above.
