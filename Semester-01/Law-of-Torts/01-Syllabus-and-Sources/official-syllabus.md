- **Evidence:** CCSU January 2026 examination schedule confirms the paper title/code; CCSU-hosted syllabus identifies the topic outline below.

## Prescribed topic map
### 1. Torts and tortious liability — introduction
Definition, nature and characteristics of tort; constituents of tort; damnum sine injuria and injuria sine damnum; mental element, including malice, negligence and motive; who may sue/who may not be sued; strict and absolute liability.

### 2. Justifications in tort
Volenti non fit injuria; necessity; inevitable accident and act of God; statutory authority.

### 3. Liability for torts committed by others
Vicarious liability; liability of the State and sovereign immunity; joint tortfeasors.

### 4. Specific torts
Negligence; nuisance; trespass; defamation.

### 5. Consumer Protection Act
Consumer definition and concept; contract of service and contract for service; enforcement of consumer rights.

## Scope safeguard
The subject covers core tort doctrine and introductory consumer protection. This study guide includes some CPA 2019 concepts (consumer, defect, deficiency, product liability and commission jurisdiction) to make the topic usable today. If the university or instructor provides a narrower exam outline, use that direction for prioritisation.
