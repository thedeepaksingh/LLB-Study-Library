## Answer checklist for a tort problem
1. Name the tort.
2. State each required element.
3. Identify evidence for and against every element.
4. Consider available defences.
5. Link the wrong to the damage/remedy.
6. Conclude with a qualified result if a material fact is missing.

For consumer disputes, separately check the definition of consumer, commercial-purpose exclusion, applicable complaint ground, limitation, proper commission and present rules.
