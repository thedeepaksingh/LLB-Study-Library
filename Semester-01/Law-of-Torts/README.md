## Exam method
Identify the protected interest, the alleged tort, each legal element, any defence, causation/remoteness and the remedy. For consumer problems, identify whether the claimant is a “consumer”, the defect/deficiency or unfair practice, the proper forum and remedy under the current Act/rules.
