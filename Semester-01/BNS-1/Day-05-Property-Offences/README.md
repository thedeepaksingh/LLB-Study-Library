# DAY-05 — Offences Against Property

**Status:** Topic roadmap; detailed lesson to be added.

## Topics in the located CCSU syllabus
- Theft
- Extortion
- Robbery
- Dacoity
- Dishonest misappropriation of property
- Criminal breach of trust
- Cheating

## Required comparison charts
- Theft versus extortion
- Robbery versus dacoity
- Theft versus dishonest misappropriation
- Criminal breach of trust versus cheating

For each offence, identify the property element, required mental state, consent or entrustment where relevant, statutory exceptions and punishment. Do not memorise section numbers without checking the current BNS text.

## Predicted/practice questions
1. Explain theft and its essential ingredients.
2. Distinguish theft, extortion and robbery.
3. Define dacoity and explain its relationship to robbery.
4. Explain dishonest misappropriation and criminal breach of trust.
5. Distinguish cheating from criminal breach of trust.

These are not verified PYQs. See the [official source register](../Bare-Act-and-References/official-sources.md).
