# DAY-06 — State, Public Tranquillity, Women and Children

**Status:** Topic roadmap; detailed lesson to be added.

## Topics in the located CCSU syllabus

### Offences against the State and public tranquillity
- Act endangering sovereignty, unity and integrity of India
- Unlawful assembly
- Rioting
- Affray

### Offences against women and children
- Rape
- Sexual harassment
- Voyeurism
- Stalking
- Dowry death
- Offences against children

## Study method
For every offence, note the exact statutory wording, ingredients, exceptions, punishment and relevant case law. Use neutral legal language, and do not rely on outdated IPC section numbers without checking the current BNS provision.

## Predicted/practice questions
1. Explain unlawful assembly and distinguish it from rioting.
2. Discuss the BNS provision on acts endangering sovereignty, unity and integrity of India.
3. Explain the statutory ingredients of dowry death.
4. Write a note on sexual harassment, voyeurism and stalking under the BNS.
5. Explain offences against children that are included in the prescribed syllabus.
