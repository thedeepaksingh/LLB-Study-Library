# CCSU BNS-1 — Syllabus and Topic Map

## Official syllabus located
- **Paper title:** Paper IV — Law of Crimes (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS))
- **Paper code shown:** K-1006
- **Official PDF:** [CCSU updated LL.B. syllabus, August 2024, via Board of Studies](https://cdn.ccsuniversity.ac.in/public/pdf/2025/08/4%20ballb%20llb%20updated%20syllabus%20august%202024%20via%20bos.pdf)

**Batch confirmation required:** The linked official syllabus is a strong source for the listed topics, but use the syllabus applicable to your admission year/programme. If CCSU has issued a later amended syllabus for your batch, that newer applicable document takes precedence.

## Topics listed in the located syllabus

### 1. General Principles of Crime
- Concept of crime
- Elements of crime
- Stages of crime
- Strict liability
- Constructive liability
- Common intention and common object

### 2. Inchoate Crime / Preliminary Offences
- Abetment
- Conspiracy
- Attempt

### 3. General Exceptions
- Mistake
- Accident
- Necessity
- Young age
- Unsoundness of mind
- Drunkenness
- Right of private defence

### 4. Specific Offences

#### A. Offences affecting the human body
- Culpable homicide
- Murder
- Homicide by rash and negligent act
- Hurt and grievous hurt
- Kidnapping and abduction
- Wrongful restraint and wrongful confinement
- Assault and criminal force

#### B. Offences against property
- Theft
- Extortion
- Robbery
- Dacoity
- Dishonest misappropriation of property
- Criminal breach of trust
- Cheating

#### C. Offences against the State and public tranquillity
- Act endangering sovereignty, unity and integrity of India
- Unlawful assembly
- Rioting and affray

#### D. Offences against women and children
- Rape
- Sexual harassment
- Voyeurism
- Stalking
- Dowry death
- Offences against children
