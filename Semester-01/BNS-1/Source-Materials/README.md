## How future uploads are handled
Use [Source Ingestion Template](SOURCE-INGESTION-TEMPLATE.md) for every new subject file. Keep three layers separate:
1. **Original/source transcript:** preserved without editorial changes.
2. **Assistant-created study notes:** explanation, cross-links and exam aids, clearly labelled as authored notes.
3. **Verification/correction log:** any law, section number, syllabus, case citation or other claim checked against an authoritative source. Never silently amend the source archive.
