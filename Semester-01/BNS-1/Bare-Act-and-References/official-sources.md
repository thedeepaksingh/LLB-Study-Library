## 3. Verification rules
- Confirm the exact syllabus for the student's programme and admission batch.
- Verify definitions, section numbers, exceptions and punishments against the official BNS text.
- For BNS/IPC comparisons, read both relevant provisions; do not assume numbering or wording is unchanged.
- Prefer official court websites or reliable law reports for case law.
- Treat search snippets as discovery aids, not substitutes for source texts.
