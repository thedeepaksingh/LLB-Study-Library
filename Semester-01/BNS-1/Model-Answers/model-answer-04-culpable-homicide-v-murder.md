# Model Answer 04 — Culpable Homicide and Murder

**Question classification:** Predicted/model question; not a verified PYQ.

## Introduction

The BNS distinguishes culpable homicide from murder by the applicable statutory mental element, circumstances and exceptions. Murder is the more serious category, but the result cannot be decided from the fact of death alone.

## Statutory provisions

- **Section 100:** defines culpable homicide.
- **Section 101:** defines murder.
- **Section 103:** punishment for murder.
- **Section 105:** punishment for culpable homicide not amounting to murder.

Check the current enacted text and relevant subsection before stating detailed ingredients or punishment.

## Analysis

To classify the offence, consider:
1. whether the accused caused the death;
2. what intention or knowledge is established;
3. which statutory clause applies;
4. whether the facts fall within a murder exception;
5. what offence remains after applying the statutory clauses and exceptions.

A high-quality answer explains the actual legal differences, rather than merely stating that murder is a subset of culpable homicide.

## Conclusion

Every murder falls within culpable homicide, but not every culpable homicide amounts to murder. The precise classification depends on the statutory text and facts.
