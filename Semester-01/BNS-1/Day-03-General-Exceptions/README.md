# DAY-03 — General Exceptions

**Status:** Topic roadmap; detailed lesson to be added.

## Topics in the located CCSU syllabus
- Mistake
- Accident
- Necessity
- Young age
- Unsoundness of mind
- Drunkenness
- Right of private defence

## Study method
For each exception, create a table with the statutory section, legal conditions, limits, relevant evidentiary/burden rules where applicable, an illustration and a practice problem. Verify all requirements against the BNS rather than relying on simplified summaries.

## Predicted/practice questions
1. Explain mistake of fact and distinguish it from mistake of law.
2. Discuss accident and necessity as general exceptions.
3. Explain the rules concerning young age and unsoundness of mind.
4. Discuss intoxication under the BNS.
5. Explain the right of private defence and its limits.

These are not verified PYQs. See the [official source register](../Bare-Act-and-References/official-sources.md).
