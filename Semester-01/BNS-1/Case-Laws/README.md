## Status and caution
The summaries are educational paraphrases, not quotations from judgments. Citations are supplied for identification, but the collection is not represented as an independently certified law-report database. Most cases predate BNS and interpreted the IPC or former evidence law; the casebook flags when the BNS text must be checked before applying a principle. New or changed provisions must be analysed from their current text, not treated as decided by an older case.
