# Practice Questions — BNS-1

Use these to check understanding and apply rules to facts. They are original practice material unless a source explicitly says otherwise.

## Day 1 — Recall and application
1. In your own words, define crime.
2. Explain the difference between actus reus and mens rea in a theft illustration.
3. Classify these as intention, preparation, attempt or commission, explaining why: planning a theft; buying tools; beginning to force a lock; successfully taking property with the required dishonest intention.
4. Give one example where criminal and civil liability may both arise.
5. Explain why the exact ingredients of the offence must be checked before deciding liability.

## Answer quality check
- Did you identify the legal concept correctly?
- Did you state the statutory ingredients where necessary?
- Did you apply the rule to the facts?
- Did you avoid assuming an outcome where facts are missing?
