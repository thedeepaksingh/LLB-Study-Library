# BNS-1 — Predicted Long-Answer Bank

**Classification:** PREDICTED — these are syllabus-based practice prompts, not verified CCSU previous-year questions.

- PREDICTED-BNS1-001 — Define crime and explain the essential elements of criminal liability.
- PREDICTED-BNS1-002 — Explain the stages of crime and distinguish preparation from attempt.
- PREDICTED-BNS1-003 — Explain actus reus and mens rea, and discuss limits to the general rule.
- PREDICTED-BNS1-004 — Explain strict liability and constructive/group liability.
- PREDICTED-BNS1-005 — Distinguish common intention from common object.
- PREDICTED-BNS1-006 — Explain abetment and its statutory modes.
- PREDICTED-BNS1-007 — Define criminal conspiracy and discuss the required agreement.
- PREDICTED-BNS1-008 — Explain attempt and its relationship to preparation.

- PREDICTED-BNS1-019 — Explain assault and criminal force.

## Property and other offences

- PREDICTED-BNS1-020 — Define theft and explain its ingredients.
- PREDICTED-BNS1-021 — Distinguish theft from extortion.
- PREDICTED-BNS1-022 — Distinguish robbery from dacoity.

- PREDICTED-BNS1-024 — Explain unlawful assembly, rioting and affray.

## Usage notes

These prompts are priorities for practice, not a forecast or guarantee of the examination paper. Questions must be matched to the syllabus applicable to the student's batch. Before writing an answer, check the exact statutory text and relevant exceptions.
