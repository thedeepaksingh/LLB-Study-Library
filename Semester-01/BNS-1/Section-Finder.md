## How to use
1. Open the official Act linked in [official sources](Bare-Act-and-References/official-sources.md).
2. Read the complete provision and any relevant definitions, exceptions or cross-references.
3. Record the ingredient in your own words without changing the legal test.
4. Link the section to the topic note and model answer.
5. Recheck any section that has been amended or interpreted in a relevant judgment before the exam.
