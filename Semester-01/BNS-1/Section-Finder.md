# BNS-1 Section Finder — Direct Learning Edition

Use this page to jump from the offence/topic to the **plain-English section explanation** and core study notes. Section titles and ingredients are summarised here; when quoting law, confirm the exact current wording in the enacted BNS. The section-wise guide itself is intended to be readable without opening external websites.

**Primary in-repository guide:** [BNS Section-Wise Learning Guide](Bare-Act-and-References/BNS-SECTION-WISE-LEARNING-GUIDE.md)

## How to use the section
1. Read the topic explanation in the linked [BNS Section-Wise Learning Guide](Bare-Act-and-References/BNS-SECTION-WISE-LEARNING-GUIDE.md).
2. Learn the section number and legal ingredients in your own words.
3. Connect the section to one case from the [case-law casebook](Case-Laws/CASE-LAW-CASEBOOK.md).
4. Practise applying each ingredient to one hypothetical from the [worked problem set](Question-Bank/EXAM-PRACTICE-PROBLEMS-WITH-WORKED-ANSWERS.md).
5. Where the law changed, a provision is new, or a commencement question matters, state that limitation rather than assuming the old IPC answer applies unchanged.
