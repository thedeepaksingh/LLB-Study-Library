## Exam method
State the legal issue, cite the relevant Contract Act section, explain the ingredients, compare any exception, apply the facts and state the result/remedy. In most long answers, distinguish **agreement**, **contract**, **void agreement**, **voidable contract** and **unenforceable agreement** accurately.
