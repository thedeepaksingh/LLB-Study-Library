# K-1005 — Syllabus and Official Sources

## Course identity
- Paper: **Contract–I (General Principles of Contract)**
- Code: **K-1005**
- University/programme: CCSU, three-year LL.B.
- Semester: 1

## Topics as listed in the CCSU syllabus
1. Purpose, evolution and scope of the law of contracts.
2. Proposal and promise, including communication of proposal, acceptance and communication of revocation of proposal and acceptance.
3. Consideration and doctrine of privity of contract.
4. Lawful object and consideration.
5. Capacity to contract: minors, persons of unsound mind and others disqualified by law; nature and effects of minor's agreements; doctrine of restitution.
6. Consent and free consent.
7. Standard-form contracts.
8. Agreements declared void and voidable under the Contract Act.
9. Contingent contracts.
10. Quasi-contracts — relations resembling those created by contracts.
11. Discharge of contracts, with special reference to frustration.
12. Remedies in the form of compensation.

## Scope boundary
K-1005 is the general-principles paper. Specific contracts such as indemnity, guarantee, bailment and agency and the law of partnership may be covered in the Act or later papers, but do not let them displace the listed K-1005 syllabus topics unless your class specifically includes them.
