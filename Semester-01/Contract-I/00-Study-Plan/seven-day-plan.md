| 7 | Quasi-contracts, integrated revision and mock | One timed long answer and four fact problems |

## Contract-problem answer checklist
1. Is there an offer or only an invitation to offer?
2. Was acceptance absolute and communicated, or was the stipulated act performed?
3. Is there consideration and a lawful object?
4. Are the parties competent and is consent free?
5. Is the agreement void, voidable, contingent or otherwise enforceable?
6. Was the contract performed, discharged, frustrated or breached?
7. What remedy follows, and what loss is legally recoverable?

Learn statutory sections with the words of the relevant rule rather than memorising section numbers alone.
