# Review and Approval Policy

## Approval rule

The repository owner retains the final decision on whether a change is merged to main.

- Work on a branch, not main.
- Open a pull request for each content or workflow change.
- Checks and review checklists are evidence of review, not proof of perfection.
- Do not merge or update main until the owner explicitly authorises that specific PR/change.
- Approval of one PR does not authorise unrelated or future changes.
- If changes are requested, revise the branch and resubmit the updated diff.
- No automatic merging.

## Review levels

### Level 1 — Structure and navigation
- File is in the correct semester/subject/day/topic location.
- Title, index and relative links work.
- Existing material is preserved.
- Question IDs and labels are consistent.

### Level 2 — Syllabus coverage
- Topic is within the applicable official syllabus or clearly marked as additional context.
- Programme, batch/session and syllabus source are recorded.
- No syllabus line was silently omitted or invented.

### Level 3 — Legal accuracy
- Each statutory section and penalty is checked in the official Act.
- Definitions, ingredients, exceptions and amendments are described accurately.
- Case citations and holdings are verified.
- Uncertainty and fact-dependence are stated honestly.

### Level 4 — Examination usefulness
- Explanation is beginner-friendly and uses simple English.
- Answers contain a definition, provision, ingredients, explanation, illustration, relevant authority and conclusion where appropriate.
- Questions are labelled as verified PYQ, predicted or practice.
- Answer key has been reviewed against the law.

### Level 5 — Final release readiness
- Internal-link check passes.
- Changed files have been inspected.
- Changelog is updated.
- Pull request includes scope, sources and known limitations.
- Owner has reviewed the final diff and explicitly authorises merge.

## Meaning of approval

Approval means the owner agrees to publish the reviewed version, not that legal or academic perfection is guaranteed. If an error is found later, fix it in a new branch and document the correction. Prefer evidence and transparent correction over claims of certainty.


## Study-first workflow

The assistant should manage repository maintenance and study-file organisation wherever available GitHub tools permit it. The owner should not be asked to manually create files, folders or branches for routine work. All BNS-1 content should remain under `Semester-01/BNS-1/`, with the completeness matrix used to track what is finished and what remains.
